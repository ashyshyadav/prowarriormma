HEAD COACH

This is COACH Mr. Aman Ansari, a certified black belt from Kukkiwon, Korea. He also holds a Master of Business Administration (MBA) from the Welingkar Institute of Management Mumbai.

He started Mixed Martial Arts training at the age of 7 under Mr. Arman Verma and has received BJJ training from Coach Ido Pariente (Israel). He played many MMA and Kick Boxing tournaments at state and national levels.

After that, he started "PRO WARRIOR MMA AND FITNESS SCIENCE" on 10th October 2014.

He has experience as an MMA coach, personal and group trainer, and has also trained army/constables. Throughout his career, he has trained over 5,000+ students.

Training is his passion, and he continues to train himself, mastering Multidimensional Mixed Martial Arts Skills.
